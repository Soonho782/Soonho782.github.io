OUR RESEARCH

Can ion channels become
therapeutic targets?

Ion channels regulate electrical and chemical signaling in sensory cells and play important roles in the detection and transmission of sensory information.

We investigate ion channels as potential therapeutic targets and explore compounds that can modulate channel activity and sensory signaling.

RESEARCH TOPIC 01

Target
Identification

Identifying ion channels involved in sensory signaling is an important step toward understanding their potential as therapeutic targets.

We investigate the functional properties of candidate channels and evaluate their roles in cellular and sensory responses.

IMAGE

RESEARCH TOPIC 02

Compound
Screening

We explore compounds that modulate ion-channel activity and investigate their effects on cellular signaling.

Functional assays can be used to identify candidate modulators and characterize their effects on channel-dependent responses.
