OUR RESEARCH

How do cells sense
mechanical forces?

Mechanical forces are fundamental biological signals that influence sensory perception and cellular function. We investigate how cells detect these physical stimuli and convert them into electrical and biochemical signals.

Our research focuses on mechanosensitive ion channels and the molecular mechanisms that regulate their activation, ion conduction, and downstream signaling.

RESEARCH TOPIC 01

Mechanosensitive
Ion Channels

Mechanosensitive ion channels directly respond to physical forces applied to the cell membrane and transform mechanical stimuli into ionic currents.

We investigate the molecular and biophysical properties of these channels to understand how mechanical information is encoded by sensory cells.

Molecular

We study the molecular mechanisms that determine mechanosensitive channel activation and function, including channel structure, membrane interactions, and cellular regulation.
