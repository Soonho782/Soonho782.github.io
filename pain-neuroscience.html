OUR RESEARCH

How does the nervous system
detect pain?

Pain begins with the detection of potentially harmful mechanical, thermal, and chemical stimuli by specialized sensory neurons.

We investigate the cellular and molecular mechanisms underlying nociception and explore how ion channels contribute to sensory signaling and pathological pain.

RESEARCH TOPIC 01

Sensory Neurons
& Nociception

Sensory neurons detect potentially damaging stimuli from the external and internal environment and convert these signals into electrical activity.

We investigate the molecular mechanisms that regulate nociceptor activation and sensory signal transmission.

IMAGE

RESEARCH TOPIC 02

Ion Channels
in Pain

Ion channels play essential roles in detecting sensory stimuli and controlling the excitability of nociceptive neurons.

Understanding these channels provides insight into the mechanisms of pain and potential strategies for controlling abnormal sensory signaling.
